== Photosynthesis ==
Photosynthesis is the process plants use to convert light energy into chemical energy. It happens in the chloroplasts. The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes and produce ATP and NADPH. The Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma and uses ATP and NADPH to build glucose from carbon dioxide.

== Python Basics ==
A list in Python is an ordered, mutable collection. A tuple is ordered but immutable. A dictionary maps keys to values and keeps insertion order. List comprehensions like [x*x for x in range(5)] create lists in one line.

== Machine Learning ==
Overfitting happens when a model memorizes training data and performs poorly on new data. Common fixes include getting more data, regularization, dropout, and early stopping. A train/validation/test split helps detect overfitting.

== Study Tips ==
The Pomodoro technique uses 25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5 minute break. After four rounds, take a longer 15 to 30 minute break. Spaced repetition means reviewing material at increasing intervals to improve long-term memory.
